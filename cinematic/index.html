A Small Room, A Bigger World

A bed beneath a mosquito net. A small table. The same walls, the same nights, and a thousand thoughts about the future.

Right now, this is just where I sleep, work, learn, and wonder where all of this is leading. There are plans that feel close enough to touch, and others that still seem impossibly far away.

I don't know which city I'll call home five years from now. I don't know exactly which opportunities I'll pursue, which ideas will work, or which unexpected turns I'll take.

But I hope that one day, when I look back at this photograph, I'll smile. Not because everything was easy here, but because so much of what came later began in a room like this.

Somewhere between where I am and where I want to be.
